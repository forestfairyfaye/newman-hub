OFFICE OF THE CHIEF CURATOR // INTERNAL MEMO

A Note on Modern Friction

This season at Galerie Newman, we are intentionally leaning into the friction between the digital and the tactile. The artists selected for the spring cycle represent a movement back toward the physical artifact.

We see it in the way Elara Vance tears her canvases before re-stitching them. We see it in the intentional "errors" of the studio process. We invite you to touch the textures (figuratively) and embrace the curated chaos.

Warmly,

Julian Newman
